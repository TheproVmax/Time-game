THE INCIDENT

What happened that night?

Investigator's note

In the early hours of 8 August 1963, a gang stopped a Royal Mail train travelling between Glasgow and London. They interfered with a railway signal, forced the train to stop, and stole bags of cash being transported on board.

The robbery became one of Britain's most famous crimes. Investigators followed clues to a farmhouse hideout, and several gang members were eventually caught.
